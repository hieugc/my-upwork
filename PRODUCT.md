# my-upwork — Product Portfolio

## Goal
Build a portfolio repository containing eight independently deployable web products plus a portfolio hub. Each product must look intentionally different and demonstrate realistic interactions without requiring production business data.

## Products
1. ChainPulse — Crypto Marketing Agency
2. OrbitOps — AI Automation Agency
3. MetricFlow — B2B SaaS
4. Maison Estate — Luxury Real Estate
5. NOMA Coffee — Cafe / Restaurant
6. AURA Commerce — DTC Ecommerce
7. Northstar Studio — Creative / Development Agency
8. LedgerX UI — Fintech / Crypto Dashboard
9. Portfolio Hub — index and case-study entry point

## Portfolio rule
No product may be a recolored clone of another. Typography, composition, component rhythm, imagery direction and signature interaction must be product-specific.

## MVP behavior
- Fully responsive desktop/tablet/mobile
- Accessible keyboard and focus behavior
- Realistic seeded/mock data
- Working navigation and major interactions
- SEO metadata, OpenGraph and structured data where relevant
- Contact/demo actions handled without persistent user data
- No fake testimonials presented as real
